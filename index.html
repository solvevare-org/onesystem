Questions

What owners ask us first

Is this only for government contracts?

No. It works for any uniform supplier with more than 1 client program: hotels, hospitals, schools, offices, restaurants, trades, security and public safety.

Do I need to be good with computers?

No. Setting up a client is 7 steps, mostly picking from lists. We set up your first client with you on a call.

Will it work with my suppliers?

Yes. SanMar, Bodek and Rhodes, Core365, Nike and Adidas catalogs are ready to load. Any other supplier can be added from a spreadsheet.

Can my client approve orders first?

Yes. You pick who can order: only the client's manager, everyone, or everyone with a manager's approval.

Does it handle allowances and monthly billing?

Yes. Set item or dollar allowances per team, then bill each client from 1 statement at the end of the month.

What if my internet goes down?

The point of sale keeps working offline, so your counter never stops. It catches up when you're back online.

What does it cost?

It depends on how many clients and locations you have. You'll get a clear price on the demo call.
